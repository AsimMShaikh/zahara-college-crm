Zahara Digital Experience Platform (ZDXP)
Product Requirements Document (Version 1.0)
1. Project Vision
Create a modern digital platform for Zahara College of Skills that goes beyond being a marketing website.
The first release is a public-facing website, but the architecture should evolve into a complete platform including CRM, marketing automation, landing pages, analytics, and administration.
The website should feel like a modern technology product rather than a traditional educational institute website.
2. Mission
Empower young women through affordable, practical, career-oriented vocational education while making it effortless for parents to trust the institute and for students to discover the right career path.
3. Product Goals

6. Core Design Philosophy
The website should not feel like it is selling courses.
It should feel like it is guiding life decisions.
Every page should answer one question before asking the visitor to take action.

29. Guiding Principles
Build for tomorrow, not just today.
Design sections, not pages.
Keep the frontend independent of the data source.
Every page should move the visitor toward a decision.
Authenticity over perfection.
Treat the website as the first application of a larger platform, not as a standalone marketing site.
